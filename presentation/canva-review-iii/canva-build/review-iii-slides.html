01

Why large messages cause trouble

A large message may need to travel in several pieces.

1 Connection setup needs large security messages.

2 An oversized UDP message can split into IP fragments.

3 One missing fragment prevents rebuilding that message.

Think of a puzzle that needs every piece.

Our test network allowed 1,500-byte IP packets. Other networks can differ.

02

Reliable setup, small voice packets

TCP for setup and control

Resends missing data so large setup messages can arrive.

UDP for the conversation

Sends small encrypted voice packets without TCP's delivery waits.

Use each transport for the job it suits.

“Hybrid” means TCP + UDP. Voice application datagrams are limited to 1,200 bytes.

03

How a call works

1 Connect and check the expected peer identity.

2 Establish a shared secret and derive the voice key.

3 Compress and encrypt the microphone audio.

4 Send, decrypt, reorder and play the voice packets.

Connect → establish key → protect voice → play

Setup: ML-KEM-768, ML-DSA-65 and HKDF-SHA256. Voice: Opus and ChaCha20-Poly1305.
